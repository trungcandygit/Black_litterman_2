### D-SR3-1 Dissent record (Phase 2A)
- item: SR3; reason_code: criterion_ambiguous
- original criterion (PC-SR3 (a)): "a stated test of discrete level shifts at the confirmation and list-publication months relative to a fitted (treated-specific) trend, with estimates and inference reported and H2 language matched to the result".
- ambiguity: "fitted (treated-specific) trend" does not say over which period the trend is fitted. The inherited criterion descends from R1-W6, whose alternative is post-announcement "gradual growth" / "steady drift" ("the event study shows a steady drift more than steps at disclosure months"). A trend fitted over the whole sample, alongside the three window dummies, is pinned by the flat pre-period and cannot represent that alternative.
- replacement criterion: level shifts at the confirmation and list dates tested against a treated-specific trend that is allowed to operate from the announcement onward (post-announcement drift), with H2 language matched to that result.
- verdict under original criterion (disclosed): FULLY_ADDRESSED. Verdict under replacement: PARTIALLY_ADDRESSED.

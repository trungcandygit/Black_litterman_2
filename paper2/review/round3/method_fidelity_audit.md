## B. Computed in the code but not described in the paper (needed for reproducibility)

1. **Trading-day grid.** The grid is the union of dates across all 405 files from 2024-08-21 (519 days). "*d*−1", "five trading days" and the 10-day blocks all refer to rows of this grid. A stock with no row on day *d*−1 has no return, and so no event, on day *d*.
2. **Event window.** Events run on rows 61 to nd−5 (Tables 1-4) and on rows 61 to nd−1 (Figure 2 / C5). Table 1 additionally requires complete returns for *d*+1..*d*+5. Tables 2-4 require only that the measure be finite, so event counts differ across tables (Table 1: 3,187; Table 2: 3,199 vs market and 3,100 vs controls).
3. **The weekly regressions use a later and shorter sample than the event tests.** Formation days run from trading day 120 to day nd−9 (`seq(120, nd-5, 5)`), while events run from day 61. The two halves of the 26-test family therefore cover different calendar spans.
4. **Weekly-regression cross-section filters and look-ahead conditioning.** The cross-section requires complete forward returns over *τ*+1..*τ*+5 (40_zoo.R:24). This conditions on future data availability, which a reader should know. Inside the lookback, the market proxy for beta, IVOL and IMOM uses weights fixed at formation day τ over the stocks of that cross-section (40_zoo.R:25).
5. **Characteristic definitions** (40_zoo.R:30-37). REV1W and REV1M are the 5-day and 20-day compounded returns. VOL, IVOL (market-model residual SD) and BETA use 60 days. DOWNVOL = $\sqrt{\text{mean}(\min(r,0)^2)}$ around zero. RANGEVOL = Parkinson over 60 days. MAX and MIN use 20 days. SKEW and KURT are 60-day moments with population SD (KURT is not excess). AMIHUD is a 60-day mean over positive-volume days. LNDVOL = log of the 60-day mean DV. DVOLCHG and TURNCHG are the ratio of the 20-day to the 60-day mean of DV and of volume. CSSPREAD is a 60-day Corwin-Schultz spread with negative two-day estimates set to 0 and no overnight adjustment. OVERNIGHT and INTRADAY are 20-day means. LIMITFREQ is the share of the last 20 days with $\lvert r\rvert \geq 6.5\%$.
6. **Quintile spreads and costs** (40_zoo.R:46-50). The spread is top minus bottom 20% (equal-weighted) of the abnormal five-day return, with at least 50 stocks. Turnover is the sum over both legs of the share of names replaced. The net spread is gross − 0.0025 × 2 × turnover, with zero cost in the first period. The *t*-statistic uses NW with 4 lags.
7. **Excluding the least liquid 20%** (40_zoo.R:44). Stocks at or below the 20th percentile of 60-day ADV at formation are dropped.
8. **First day of a streak** (47:22-24). An exact (or rule-based) hit counts as a streak start when the previous grid day was not a hit. Hits on the first row of the event window are excluded because their previous day is not observed.
9. **Locked all day** (47:20). Event with O ≥ H and H ≤ L (1e-9 tolerance), that is O = H = L = C.
10. **Attention proxies** (48:55-59). Volume ratio = $V_d / \text{mean}(V_{d-60..d-1})$. Prior 20-day return = $C_{d-1}/C_{d-21}-1$. Terciles are cut within each event type's sample.
11. **Week and block clusters** (48:13). ISO week (`%G-%V`) of the event date d; block = floor(row/10) on the grid, so blocks are fixed in calendar time and do not start at the first event.
12. **The 20-observation minimum in `cl2`.** Any subsample with fewer than 20 events reports NA.
13. **Characteristic p-values use a *t*(78) reference, and event p-values in the family use the normal.** The two parts of the 26-test family therefore use different reference distributions (item 18; item 13).
14. **Computed but not reported** (acceptable, but list them in the replication README so the code's outputs make sense): the equal-weighted and beta-adjusted benchmarks (`gap_ew`, `cc1_beta`, …), the effective number of tests (C15 rows 1-2), C12 attrition sensitivity, C19 specified-week split, the C15 normal-reference bound of 816, the "README-style" and complete-returns groups in C11, and the "not locked all day" subsample.

## C. Described in the paper but not computed (or computed differently)

1. **Survival rule against the full-sample sign** (l.115). The code checks sign agreement between the discovery and confirmation halves instead (item 22).
2. **"Date clustering without G/(G−1)" as a description of Table 1** (l.103 with the l.136 caption). Table 1's date column includes the factor (items 13 and 14).
3. **"Exact limit hit" as a subset of ceiling/floor closes** (Table 2 layout). The code does not restrict exact hits to the event rule, so 6 ceiling and 3 floor exact hits fall outside it, by the C11 count arithmetic (item 5).
4. **"79 weeks".** These are five-trading-day periods, not calendar weeks (item 17).
5. **"Weights through day *d*+1" for Table 1 as a whole.** The five-day and *d*+2..*d*+5 rows use weights through *d*+*k* (item 4).
6. **The "next five days' abnormal return" in Eq. (7).** It is not the CAR of Eq. (4): the benchmark is a buy-and-hold weighted average over the cross-section stocks (item 17).

Nothing described in Section 4 is missing from the code entirely. The plan deviations listed in "Analysis plan" are consistent with the code (median split: 40_zoo.R:64 and 48:18; *d*+1..*d*+5 horizon; quintiles: 40_zoo.R:47).

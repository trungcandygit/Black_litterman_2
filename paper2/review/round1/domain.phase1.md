## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor, mandatory, owned by the methodology seat): the study design, handling of data, reporting of statistics and the means for others to reproduce the work must reach the standard a peer-reviewed finance venue expects. From a domain-accuracy standpoint I do not score this dimension. I only note that the empirical design must be capable of supporting the economic interpretation the domain literature would demand.

D2 (domain_accuracy, mandatory, owned by me): the claims made must agree with the current state of evidence in the field, earlier work must be represented faithfully and attributed to its true source, and domain terminology, institutional facts and reported results must be free of factual error. For a market-microstructure paper this means the trading-rule facts of the exchange are sourced rather than assumed, the theoretical and empirical price-limit literature is covered, and the reading of the results is distinguished from rival explanations.

D3 (argumentative_coherence, mandatory, owned by the devil's advocate with the methodology seat also eligible): the central thesis must be internally consistent, the evidence must actually support the stated conclusions, and no fallacy may undermine the main argument. From my vantage I only watch whether the economic story is logically compatible with what the field knows, and I leave the scoring of this dimension to the seats it names.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance, high priority, owned by the perspective seat): framing, definitions and implications should be accessible to readers from adjacent fields and any interdisciplinary claim must be substantiated. This sits outside my remit and I do not plan a score for it.

D5 (writing_and_structure, normal priority, owned by the journal-fit seat): organisation, clarity of exposition, quality of figures and tables, and conformity to venue conventions. This is not mine to score; I would only flag terminology that is used loosely as it bears on D2.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution, mandatory, owned by the journal-fit seat): the manuscript must suit the configured venue and make an original, significant contribution for its readers. The venue is generic here, so I treat contribution as the journal-fit seat's call and only supply, under D2, an accurate account of what is and is not new relative to prior literature.

## Scoring Plan

### D2: domain_accuracy

dimension_id: D2
what_to_look_for: Whether each institutional and rule-based fact about the exchange is tied to a primary or official source and is correct for the sample period (reference-price rule, limit width, tick sizes, opening call auction, session and settlement rules, short-sale constraints, rule changes); whether the price-limit literature (theory such as Brennan 1986 and later models, empirical work on Asian and Chinese A-share markets, Vietnamese studies) and the overnight versus intraday return literature are covered and correctly attributed, with original sources rather than secondhand citations; whether the economic reading of the headline results is separated from rival explanations (magnet effect, delayed price discovery, news selection and overnight information, attention, liquidity provision, momentum, news clustering); whether claims of profitability or policy implications stay within what the evidence supports, including executability at the opening auction; and whether the status of any reference that is unverified or not peer reviewed is disclosed and its use proportionate.
what_triggers_block: A load-bearing domain claim or institutional fact is wrong, unsourced and assumed, or contradicted by exchange documentation or established literature, so that the central interpretation or the stated contribution is not supported as written, and the correction would change the reading of the main result rather than only its wording.
what_triggers_warn: Domain coverage or attribution is incomplete without overturning the main claim: relevant strands of price-limit, overnight-return or regional literature are missing or cited secondhand, one or more rival explanations are named but not separated from the favoured one, institutional detail is partly sourced, or policy and profitability statements go modestly beyond the evidence.
what_triggers_fatal: The paper's headline domain claim rests on a mischaracterised or unverifiable trading rule, or on an interpretation that the established literature already refutes or that the paper's own evidence cannot distinguish from a mechanical or news-driven alternative, and no available revision of the text alone could rescue the contribution.

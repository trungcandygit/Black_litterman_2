## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor, mandatory, methodology seat only): This asks whether the study design, handling of data, reporting of statistics, and the means given for others to reproduce the work reach the standard a peer-reviewed journal in the field would expect. From a cross-disciplinary standpoint I do not score it, but I note that its outcome determines how far an outside reader can trust any behavioral or practical reading built on top.

D2 (domain_accuracy, mandatory, domain seat only): This asks whether the claims agree with current evidence in the subject area, whether earlier work is represented faithfully, and whether domain terminology and results are free of factual error. It is owned by the domain specialist; I read it only as the foundation on which adjacent-field readers would rely.

D3 (argumentative_coherence, mandatory, devil's-advocate and methodology seats): This asks whether the central thesis hangs together, whether the evidence offered actually supports the claims, and whether any flawed reasoning undermines the main argument. I do not score it, but I will keep in view that an interpretation looks different to an outsider when the argument's links are weak.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance, high priority, perspective seat): This is my dimension. It asks whether the framing, definitions, and stated implications can be followed by readers from neighboring fields, and whether any claims that reach across disciplines (behavioral, welfare, regulatory, research-practice) are actually backed by evidence rather than only asserted. I will judge it as an outsider who must be able to verify what is claimed without specialist insider knowledge.

D5 (writing_and_structure, normal priority, editor-in-chief seat): This asks about organization of the manuscript, clarity of exposition, quality of figures and tables, and fit with venue conventions. It belongs to the editor seat; I treat it as outside my scoring remit.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution, mandatory, editor-in-chief seat): This asks whether the manuscript suits the configured venue and offers an original, significant contribution for its readership. It belongs to the editor seat, and with no target venue fixed I will not assume venue criteria.

## Scoring Plan

### D4: cross_disciplinary_relevance

dimension_id: D4
what_to_look_for: Whether each interdisciplinary claim (behavioral mechanism, investor welfare or protection, regulatory meaning, research-practice credibility) is supported by stated evidence or an explicit test rather than assertion; whether key terms and institutional or procedural facts are defined or sourced so a reader from an adjacent field can follow and verify them; whether any process label (for example a registration, replication, or independence claim) is verifiable by an outsider through an independent record; whether use of automated or AI tooling and the evidence trail for it are disclosed well enough for independent replication; whether the practical or welfare conclusion is separated from a statistical association and its limits stated.
what_triggers_block: A central interdisciplinary or practical conclusion rests on a mechanism or process label that is asserted but neither tested nor externally verifiable, and the text gives an outside reader no way to check it, so the cross-field claim cannot be accepted as written.
what_triggers_warn: Cross-field implications are stated with some support but with material gaps, such as undefined jargon, unsourced institutional facts, thin tooling or provenance disclosure, or a welfare or regulatory reading that goes beyond what the evidence shows; the claims are repairable by added caveats, sourcing, or disclosure.

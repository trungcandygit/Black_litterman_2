## Contract Paraphrase

D1 methodology_rigor (mandatory, methodology seat only): From an adversarial standpoint, this dimension asks whether the study's design, data handling, statistical reporting and reproducibility affordances would survive a hostile referee at a peer-reviewed venue. I do not score it, but I will treat any gap between what is claimed and what the design can actually identify as a candidate counter-argument, and I will report such gaps as findings if the evidence supports them.

D2 domain_accuracy (mandatory, domain seat only): This dimension asks whether claims match current domain evidence, prior work is represented faithfully, and domain terminology and institutional facts are free of error. I do not score it. As a stress-tester I will note where a claim leans on an institutional or literature premise that is assumed rather than sourced, since an unsupported premise can break an argument chain even when the arithmetic is sound.

D3 argumentative_coherence (mandatory, owned by the devil's advocate, shared with methodology): This is my scored dimension. It asks whether the central thesis is internally consistent, whether the evidence offered actually supports each claim, and whether any fallacy undermines the core argument. I read it adversarially: construct the strongest rival explanation, test whether the conclusion outruns the evidence, look for contradictions between sections, for selective use of evidence, and for labels or framings that assert more than the supporting material warrants.

D4 cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, perspective seat only): This dimension asks whether framing, definitions and implications are accessible to adjacent-field readers and whether interdisciplinary claims are substantiated. I do not score it. I will at most flag a stakeholder or reader group whose absence weakens an inference, without elaborating what that group would say.

D5 writing_and_structure (normal priority, editor-in-chief seat only): This dimension covers organisation, clarity of exposition, figure and table quality, and venue conventions. I do not score it. I will only mention presentation where it obscures a logical step in the argument.

D6 venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, editor-in-chief seat only): This dimension asks whether the manuscript fits the configured venue and makes an original, significant contribution for its readership. I do not score it. As a challenger I will ask the so-what question about incremental contribution, but any verdict on venue fit belongs to the editor-in-chief seat.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Whether each headline conclusion follows from the evidence offered, with every headline number traceable to a table or figure anchor and its dependence structure; whether the thesis and its supporting sections are mutually consistent; whether the strongest rival explanation (including selection of what is observed, timing and information-flow alternatives, and inference that ignores clustered or cross-sectional dependence) is addressed on the authors' own evidence; whether labels such as pre-registered or verified are backed by something checkable; whether cited institutional or literature premises are sourced rather than assumed; and whether claim scope exceeds what the sample and design support.
what_triggers_block: A main conclusion that does not follow from the presented evidence, or an evidence-conclusion mismatch where the authors' own reported results fail to support the stated claim at the stated strength, or a rival explanation that is more parsimonious and fits the reported results at least as well but is neither addressed nor ruled out, so the central claim stands unsupported until the argument is repaired.
what_triggers_warn: Claims worded more strongly than the evidence allows without invalidating the core thesis, such as an unverifiable methodological label, an unsourced institutional premise carrying part of the argument, an inference procedure whose dependence assumptions are stated vaguely, selective emphasis of favourable results, or a rival explanation acknowledged but only partly addressed, where tightening scope or adding analysis would restore coherence.
what_triggers_fatal: A foundational premise of the paper's argument is demonstrably false or self-contradicted by the manuscript's own reported results, or the central thesis is internally contradictory, such that no revision short of reconceiving the argument can restore coherence.
